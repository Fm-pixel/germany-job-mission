I want you to build a private personal tool for me.

This is NOT a commercial SaaS product and NOT an immigration agency.

I want to use this tool to help my family members, friends and other people I personally want to help move to Germany legally for work.

## THE MISSION

The goal is simple:

HELP A PERSON OUTSIDE GERMANY FIND A REAL GERMAN JOB AND, WHEN POSSIBLE, ARRIVE IN GERMANY WITH A REAL EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT.

I do not want the tool to simply tell someone about the Chancenkarte.

I want it to actively help me find the best realistic pathway for each person.

The ideal journey is:

PERSON ABROAD → CREATE PROFILE → UNDERSTAND THEIR SKILLS → IDENTIFY SUITABLE GERMAN JOBS → IDENTIFY SUITABLE GERMAN COMPANIES → MATCH PERSON ↔ COMPANY/JOB → PREPARE APPLICATION → CONTACT/APPLY TO EMPLOYER → INTERVIEW → EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT → DETERMINE CORRECT IMMIGRATION PATHWAY → VISA APPLICATION → TRAVEL TO GERMANY

## IMPORTANT

The system must NOT promise that somebody will receive a visa or job.
It should maximize the person's chances by doing the preparation, matching, research and application work efficiently.
All immigration information must come from current authoritative German sources.
The tool must never recommend illegal immigration, fake documents, fake qualifications or false information.

# 1. PEOPLE I WANT TO HELP

I should be able to create multiple candidate profiles.

Example: Candidate: Jean · Country: Rwanda · Profession: Electrician · Education: Vocational qualification · Experience: 5 years · German: A2 · English: Good · Goal: Find electrician job in Germany · Location preference: Any German state · Willing to relocate: Yes · Salary expectation: Flexible

The system then creates a complete Germany employment profile.

# 2. CANDIDATE PROFILE

Create a very simple profile builder.

Personal: Name, Country, City, Contact information, Age
Professional: Current profession, Previous professions, Years of experience, Skills, Industries, Current employer, Previous employers
Education: School, University, Vocational training, Degree, Certificates, Graduation year, Country
Languages: German level, English level, French level, Other languages
Germany preferences: Preferred occupation, Preferred cities, Preferred states, Willing to relocate, Full-time/part-time, Salary expectation, Earliest availability
Documents: CV, certificates, diploma, references, language certificate, passport

Do NOT require unnecessary sensitive information.

# 3. CV ANALYSIS

Allow me to upload a candidate's CV. The AI should extract: profession, skills, education, experience, years of experience, languages, certificates, industries, job titles. Then create a structured candidate profile automatically. Never invent information. If something is unclear, mark it: "Needs confirmation."

# 4. GERMANY JOB MATCHING

This is the heart of the application. For each candidate, search for suitable jobs in Germany. Consider: qualifications, experience, profession, skills, German level, English level, education, location, salary, willingness to relocate.

Rank jobs: 95% MATCH / 87% MATCH / 76% MATCH. For every job explain WHY THIS JOB MATCHES, e.g.:

87% match
✓ 4 years electrician experience
✓ vocational qualification
✓ relocation possible
✓ German A2 acceptable according to vacancy
⚠ employer requests B1

Then provide "Recommended action": APPLY

# 5. COMPANY DISCOVERY

Do not only search job boards. Identify German companies that employ the candidate's profession (for an electrician: electrical contractors, construction companies, industrial companies, facility management companies, renewable-energy companies). Build a company list. For each company: Company name, Industry, Location, Website, Careers page, Relevant vacancies, Contact information if publicly available, Application method. Do not fabricate contact information.

# 6. CONTACTING COMPANIES

For each suitable company/job create: German application email, English application email, Cover letter, Short LinkedIn-style message where appropriate. Based ONLY on the candidate's real information. Example subject: "Bewerbung als Elektriker – Berufserfahrung aus Ruanda". Allow me to review before sending.

IMPORTANT: Do not automatically send large numbers of unsolicited emails. Create a review/approval step showing COMPANY, POSITION, WHY MATCH, EMAIL, MESSAGE, ATTACHMENTS with buttons APPROVE & SEND / EDIT / SKIP.

# 7. EMAIL INTEGRATION

Design so I can connect my email account. The system should: prepare emails, attach candidate CV, attach relevant certificates, send only after my approval, track sent applications, store sent messages, track replies where technically possible. Never send an email without my approval unless I explicitly enable an automation rule.

# 8. APPLICATION TRACKER

Every application becomes a record. Statuses: Potential, Prepared, Approved, Applied, Employer viewed, Reply received, Interview, Second interview, Offer, Contract, Rejected, No response. Show on a dashboard, e.g. Jean — Electrician: Applications 23, Sent 17, Replies 5, Interviews 3, Offers 1, Contract Pending.

# 9. FOLLOW-UP SYSTEM

If a company does not respond after a reasonable period, remind me ("Follow up with Müller Elektrotechnik — Application sent 8 days ago — Suggested action: SEND FOLLOW-UP"). Generate the follow-up email automatically. I approve before sending.

# 10. INTERVIEW ASSISTANT

When a company responds positively, create an interview preparation area based on the actual job description: likely interview questions, suggested answers based on the candidate's real experience, German vocabulary, technical vocabulary, questions the candidate should ask the employer. Do not invent experience.

# 11. CONTRACT STAGE

Allow me to upload an employment contract or job offer. Extract: employer, job title, salary, working hours, location, contract duration, start date, probation, other important terms. Then tell me "These are the important points to verify." Do NOT act as a lawyer. Do NOT declare the contract legally valid.

# 12. IMMIGRATION PATHWAY

Only after a genuine job opportunity exists, determine the likely immigration pathway. Consider: qualified-worker residence pathway, EU Blue Card, vocational training, Opportunity Card, recognition-related pathways, other applicable employment pathways. Must NOT assume everyone needs the Chancenkarte.

Display RECOMMENDED PATHWAY with Why (qualification, job offer, salary, profession, recognition, language, other requirements), then DOCUMENTS REQUIRED and NEXT STEPS. Always link/cite authoritative German government information. Clearly distinguish CONFIRMED REQUIREMENT from LIKELY / NEEDS CONFIRMATION from USER-SPECIFIC INFORMATION.

# 13. VISA CHECKLIST

Once the pathway is identified, create a personalized checklist (Passport, Employment contract, Qualification documents, Qualification recognition, CV, Language certificate, Employer information, Visa application, Appointment). Each item: Status, Owner, Deadline, Notes, Upload.

# 14. RECOGNITION ASSISTANT

For professions where recognition may matter, create a workflow determining: profession, qualification, issuing country, likely competent authority, whether recognition may be required, documents needed, official source. Never make a definitive legal determination unless supported by the official source.

# 15. CHANCENKARTE

Create a Chancenkarte assessment as ONE MODULE. Calculate whether the person appears to meet current requirements based on official information. Show the reasoning (Qualification ✓, Language ✓, Points 6+, Financial requirement ⚠ Needs evidence). Result: "Your information suggests that you may qualify, but final eligibility must be confirmed by the German authorities." Never say "Visa guaranteed."

# 16. "FASTEST REALISTIC PATH" FEATURE

Big button: FIND MY FASTEST REALISTIC PATH TO GERMANY. Analyse the candidate and output ranked paths (Direct employment / Recognition + employment / Opportunity Card / Vocational training) each with BEST OPTION / POSSIBLE / NOT CURRENTLY RECOMMENDED, reason and action. Optimize for LEGAL, REALISTIC, FAST, EMPLOYMENT-FIRST.

# 17. PERSONAL DASHBOARD FOR ME

"PEOPLE I'M HELPING": one card per person (name, profession, country, applications, interviews, offers). Clicking a person opens their complete journey.

# 18. PRIORITY QUEUE

"WHAT SHOULD I DO TODAY?" — 🔴🟠🟢 prioritized actions likely to move someone closer to employment (follow up with employers, approve applications, upload missing certificate, prepare interview, check visa documents).

# 19. AI "GERMANY JOB AGENT"

A natural-language assistant: "Find electrician jobs for Jean in Germany", "Find companies in Bavaria that employ industrial electricians", "Prepare applications for the five strongest matches", "Show me which applications need follow-up", "Jean got an offer. What should we do next?", "Aline received this email. Explain what the employer wants." Executes actions only when permitted; consequential actions (sending) require my confirmation.

# 20. JOB SEARCH SOURCES

Pluggable sources: official German employment services, company career pages, reputable job platforms, other legitimate public sources. Every job stores: source, original URL, date discovered, date checked. Do not create fake vacancies. Do not claim a vacancy is still active without checking.

# 21. SCAM PROTECTION

Flag: "Pay money to guarantee job", "Pay for visa guarantee", "Send passport to unknown recruiter", "Unrealistic salary", "Recruiter asking for unusual payment", "Guaranteed Germany visa". Tell the user to verify suspicious claims through official sources.

# 22. PRIVATE TOOL

Do NOT build: public pricing, subscriptions, marketing funnels, payment system, public marketplace, multi-tenant SaaS. Build: secure login, my candidates, my applications, my documents, my employer research, my tasks. Architecture clean enough to become a larger product later.

A powerful personal command center. Not a government website, not a recruitment scam website, not a generic CRM. Clean, premium, professional, trustworthy, simple. Main dashboard "MY GERMANY JOB MISSION": People, Active applications, Interviews, Offers, Contracts, then TODAY'S PRIORITIES.

Collections: candidates, candidate_profiles, education, qualifications, work_experience, languages, documents, companies, jobs, job_sources, job_matches, applications, application_messages, emails, interviews, offers, contracts, visa_pathways, visa_requirements, visa_assessments, tasks, notes, sources, audit_logs. Clean, scalable relationships.

Daily: search new matching jobs, check existing job links, identify applications requiring follow-up, notify me of employer responses.
